RESUSCITATION ROOM - INITIAL ASSESSMENT

Patient brought in by ambulance, unwell for several hours, found collapsed at
home by a neighbour.

On arrival:
HR 210 bpm
BP 88/44 mmHg
Blood glucose 612 mg/dL
T 38.9 C
RR 32 per minute

Appears dehydrated with dry mucous membranes. Kussmaul breathing noted. Drowsy
but rousable to voice.

Impression: probable diabetic ketoacidosis with haemodynamic compromise and a
narrow-complex tachycardia.
Plan: fluid resuscitation, fixed-rate insulin infusion, urgent venous gas and
ketones, continuous cardiac monitoring, critical care referral.
