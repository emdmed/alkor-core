WARD ROUND - DAY 3

Seen this morning on the medical ward. The patient looks better than yesterday
and managed to sit out in the chair for an hour. Appetite still poor.

On examination the blood pressure was 106 over 68 and the heart rate had settled
to 88 beats per minute, down from the tachycardia recorded on admission. He was
breathing 18 times a minute at rest and was comfortable. Temperature this morning
was 37.1 degrees, so the fever has broken. Oxygen saturation of 94 per cent while
on room air, which is where he was before admission.

Abdomen soft and non-tender. Bowel sounds present.

Continue current antibiotics. Consider discharge tomorrow if he remains afebrile.
