WARD ROUND - DAY 4

Continues to improve. She spent most of yesterday out in the chair and walked
the length of the bay with a frame in the afternoon.

Oxygen has been stopped and she is maintaining saturations within her target
range on air, including after walking. This is the first day of the admission on
air.

The five-day prednisolone course finishes tomorrow. Oral clarithromycin and
doxycycline continue and are due to finish at the end of the week. Nebulisers
have been reduced to as-required and her usual inhalers have been restarted
today, as documented on day two: tiotropium 18 micrograms once daily, and
beclometasone with formoterol two puffs twice daily. Her inhaler technique was
checked by the ward pharmacist and was good.

Alendronic acid was given on Sunday as due and she remained upright for the
required period without difficulty. Omeprazole continues.

Bloods: C-reactive protein 41. All other results are unremarkable and daily
bloods can now stop.

The stair assessment has been completed by the therapy team and she managed a
flight with one rail and supervision. They are happy for her to go home with a
package of care for one visit a day for two weeks, which has been requested.

Her daughter has been updated and can collect her.

Plan: discharge planned for tomorrow if she remains stable overnight and the
package of care is confirmed. Discharge summary to be prepared today.

The ward pharmacist has reconciled the discharge medication against the repeat
prescription brought in on admission and has flagged nothing further. The
allergy to penicillin is recorded on the electronic chart and on the front of
the paper notes. A copy of the discharge letter will go to the general
practitioner and to the respiratory clinic that has followed her since 2014.
