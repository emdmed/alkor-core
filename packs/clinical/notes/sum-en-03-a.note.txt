MINOR INJURIES UNIT

Inverted her right ankle playing football yesterday evening. Able to weight-bear four steps
in the department.

Previously well, no past medical history of note. Takes no regular medication. She has been
using paracetamol bought over the counter as required for the pain.

Examination: swelling over the lateral malleolus, no bony tenderness. Ottawa rules
negative, so no radiograph.

Diagnosis: lateral ligament sprain of the right ankle. Referred to physiotherapy.
