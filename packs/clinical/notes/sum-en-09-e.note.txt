DISCHARGE SUMMARY

Admitted with community-acquired pneumonia on a background of chronic
obstructive pulmonary disease and an infective exacerbation. Treated with
clarithromycin, initially intravenous and then oral, with doxycycline, and a
five-day course of prednisolone which has now been completed. Controlled oxygen
was required for the first four days and she has been on air since.

She has been reviewed by physiotherapy and occupational therapy. A stair
assessment has been completed and passed. A package of care of one visit a day
for two weeks has been arranged and starts tomorrow.

Medication on discharge: tiotropium 18 micrograms once daily, beclometasone with
formoterol two puffs twice daily, salbutamol as required, alendronic acid 70 mg
once weekly on a Sunday, and omeprazole 20 mg each morning. She is to complete
two further days of oral clarithromycin 500 mg twice daily and doxycycline 100
mg once daily and then stop both. The prednisolone course is finished and is not
to be continued.

She remains allergic to penicillin.

Outstanding at discharge: a pneumococcal vaccination is to be arranged by the
general practitioner, and a respiratory clinic appointment in six weeks has been
requested with a repeat chest radiograph to confirm resolution of the
consolidation. Neither has been done.

Advice given about when to seek help, and a rescue pack was discussed but not
issued.

Social circumstances at discharge: she lives alone in a first-floor flat with a
flight of stairs and no lift, and her daughter visits at weekends. She was
independent in all activities of daily living before this admission and is close
to that again. The package of care is a temporary measure for two weeks and is
to be reviewed by the community team rather than continued indefinitely.

Follow-up arrangements and outstanding items are as listed above.
