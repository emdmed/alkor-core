GENERAL PRACTICE - TELEPHONE CONSULTATION

40-year-old patient calling about difficulty sleeping over the past two months.

Describes taking upwards of an hour to fall asleep, with early waking around
four in the morning. Denies low mood, anhedonia or thoughts of self-harm. Work
stress has increased since a change of role. Caffeine intake is two coffees
before midday. No alcohol most days.

No physical examination performed - this was a telephone appointment.

Assessment: primary insomnia, likely situational.
Plan: sleep hygiene advice given. Trial of melatonin 2 mg at night. Review in
four weeks, in person, if no improvement.
