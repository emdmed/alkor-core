RESPIRATORY CLINIC - LETTER TO THE GENERAL PRACTITIONER

Thank you for referring this patient, whom I saw in clinic today.

On examination: BP 118/76 mmHg*, pulse 88 bpm and regular, SpO₂ 96 % on air,
temperature 37·2 °C. Weight 74·8 kg†, down from the figure in your letter.

* taken after ten minutes seated; the first reading, 134/88 mmHg, was taken on
arrival before she had settled.

† your letter of 14 April gives 78·1 kg and the practice nurse's card from
March gives 79·6 kg; both predate today and neither was measured here.

Peak flow was around 320 L/min, roughly 70 % of predicted. She used 2 L/min of
oxygen during the six-minute walk, which is a flow rate and not a saturation.

I have increased her inhaled steroid and will review her in two months.
