GENERAL PRACTICE - SAME DAY APPOINTMENT

Attends with a cough for five days, productive of green sputum, no
breathlessness at rest and no chest pain.

On examination the chest is clear. BP 142/91 mmHg, HR 67 bpm, temperature
37.6 C.

No wheeze. Throat mildly injected. No lymphadenopathy.

Advised simple analgesia and fluids. Safety-netted to return if the fever
persists beyond three more days or breathlessness develops.
