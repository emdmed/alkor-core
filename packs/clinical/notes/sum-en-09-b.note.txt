WARD ROUND - DAY 2

Reviewed on the consultant round. She had a poor night with frequent coughing
but did not require any increase in her oxygen. She is eating a little and
drinking well. The daughter visited yesterday evening and was updated.

Still breathless on speaking in full sentences, though better than on admission.
The wheeze has softened. The right basal crackles persist. No new findings on
examination and no peripheral oedema. She has passed urine normally and her
bowels opened this morning.

Repeat bloods show the C-reactive protein has fallen to 168 and the white cell
count is coming down. Renal function remains normal. The blood cultures taken in
the emergency department have grown nothing at 24 hours. Sputum has been sent
and is with the laboratory.

The physiotherapist has seen her and has started a sputum clearance regime twice
daily. She has been sat out in the chair for an hour this morning, which she
tolerated, and the therapy team will assess her mobility properly tomorrow when
she is less breathless.

Medication reviewed on the round. The intravenous clarithromycin is continued
for now and will be switched to oral when she has been afebrile for
twenty-four hours. Doxycycline continues. Prednisolone continues, day two of
five. Nebulisers are being given four-hourly and she is managing them well. Her
usual inhalers are held while she is on nebulisers, which is documented so that
they are restarted rather than forgotten. Alendronic acid is due on Sunday and
the nursing staff have been asked to sit her upright for it.

Omeprazole continues at her usual dose.

Plan: continue the current treatment. Repeat the chest radiograph if she is not
improving by day four. Daily bloods. Occupational therapy referral for a home
assessment given that she lives alone with stairs.

Nutrition and hydration reviewed. She is on a normal diet with supplements
offered between meals, and the fluid balance chart shows an adequate intake over
the last twenty-four hours. Pressure areas are intact and she is repositioning
herself in bed without prompting. The falls risk assessment has been completed
and she has been given a call bell within reach.
